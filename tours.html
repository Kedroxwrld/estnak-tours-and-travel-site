Tour Starter

Don't see your trip? Start from scratch.

Tour Starter is our custom-build service: you bring the dates, the budget and the daydream. We bring the routing, the lodges and the local knowledge. No template required.

  1. 1

    Tell us the shape of it

    Dates, travellers, budget range and the two or three things you really want to see.

  2. 2

    We design, you react

    Within two working days you get a route, a budget and honest notes on what's worth the splurge.

  3. 3

    Refine, confirm, go

    Two rounds of changes are included. Then we book, brief you and stay reachable throughout.
