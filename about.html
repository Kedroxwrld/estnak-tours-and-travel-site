Company objectives

What we aim to achieve.

Objective 1: To carry on the business of tour operators and travel agents and to provide travel-related services locally and internationally.

Objective 2: To organize, arrange and conduct tourist trips, excursions, safaris and sightseeing tours within Uganda and other countries.

Objective 3: To provide transportation and ground transfer services for tourists, travellers, companies and other clients.

Objective 4: To arrange and facilitate hotel, lodge, resort and other accommodation bookings for travellers and tourists.

Objective 5: To provide airline, bus, train and other travel ticketing and reservation services, subject to applicable laws and regulations.

What we stand for

Four things we won't compromise on.

They're not posters on the wall. They're the questions we ask before we book anything.

Local first

Guides, cooks, drivers and camps from the communities we visit. The money stays closer to the place.

Fair by default

Fair crew pay, transparent pricing and no commission games. If we can't explain a cost, we don't charge it.

Detail obsessed

The window seat, the early start, the birthday cake at the right moment. Small things are the trip.

Honest advice

If a trip isn't right for you, we'll say so. A great referral beats a bad booking every time.

The humans
