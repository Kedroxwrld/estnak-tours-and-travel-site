Our purpose

Travel support built around your plans.

Estnak Tours and Travel is a Kampala-based travel agency for local and international journeys. We bring tour operations, ticketing, accommodation and transport arrangements together so you can discuss your trip with one team.

Our objectives guide how we work:

  • Organize tourist trips, excursions, safaris and sightseeing within Uganda and other countries.

  • Arrange airline and other ticketing, plus hotel, lodge, resort and accommodation bookings.

  • Provide transportation and ground transfers for tourists, travellers, companies and other clients.

  • Offer travel-related services locally and internationally, subject to applicable laws and regulations.

What guides us

Four values at the heart of our work.

These principles shape the questions we ask and the options we discuss before a booking is made.

Local first

We look for local guides, drivers, accommodation and experiences that reflect the places we visit.

Clear pricing

We explain what is included, what is arranged on request and which costs may change before you confirm.

Responsible travel

We consider wildlife, local communities and the environment when discussing routes and suppliers.

Personal support

You can speak directly with our team about your itinerary, documents, transfers and booking questions.
