Questions

Useful answers before you travel.

A few practical details to help you plan. If your question is not covered here, contact our team.

Ask on WhatsApp
Can you create a custom trip?

Yes. Share your preferred dates, destinations, travel style and budget, and we can shape an itinerary around your plans. Availability and pricing are confirmed in your written quote.

How do gorilla trekking permits work?

Gorilla permits are required for trekking and must be available for your chosen date. Availability and fees are set by the relevant authorities and can change; they must be confirmed before travel. We can help you explore options, but a permit is not guaranteed.

Do I need a visa to visit Uganda?

Visa requirements depend on your nationality, travel document and itinerary. Check the latest rules with official immigration authorities before booking or travelling. We can provide visa assistance for your plans.

When is the best time to visit Uganda?

Uganda can be visited throughout the year. The best timing depends on the destinations and activities you prefer, as rainfall and conditions vary by region. We can help you choose dates for your route.

What is included and excluded in a quote?

Each written quote lists the services, accommodation, transport, meals, fees and activities included, along with any exclusions. Items such as international flights, visas, insurance and personal expenses are included only when specifically stated.

What are the payment and change terms?

Payment schedules and change or cancellation terms are confirmed in your written quote. They can vary with the itinerary and supplier requirements, so review the details before agreeing to a booking.

How can I contact Estnak Tours and Travel?

Call 0769 532299, message us on WhatsApp or email estnaktoursandtravel@gmail.com. Our office hours are Monday–Saturday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM EAT; Sunday is closed.
