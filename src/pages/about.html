How we began

It started with one borrowed Land Cruiser.

In 2014, Daniel drove three friends to Bwindi in a borrowed Land Cruiser and a cooler full of rolex. By the time they came back, two of them had asked him to plan their honeymoons. That was the whole business plan, really: take care of people properly, and they'll tell others.

Twelve years later we're a team of 18 in Kampala, working with more than 60 guides, drivers and lodge partners across East Africa and beyond. We still answer our own phones. We still read every review. And we still think the best part of the job is the moment a guest sees something they'll never forget.

What we stand for

Four things we won't compromise on.

They're not posters on the wall. They're the questions we ask before we book anything.

Local first

Guides, cooks, drivers and camps from the communities we visit. The money stays closer to the place.

Fair by default

Fair crew pay, transparent pricing and no commission games. If we can't explain a cost, we don't charge it.

Detail obsessed

The window seat, the early start, the birthday cake at the right moment. Small things are the trip.

Honest advice

If a trip isn't right for you, we'll say so. A great referral beats a bad booking every time.

The long way round

A dozen years, one direction.

Slow growth, deliberate choices and a lot of miles on Ugandan roads.

  1. 2014

    The first Bwindi run

    Daniel takes three friends to see gorillas. Two honeymoons and a family reunion follow within the year.

  2. 2017

    Our first lodge partnerships

    Long-term agreements with community-owned camps in Bwindi and Queen Elizabeth National Park.

  3. 2020

    The Estnak Community Fund

    We commit 1% of every booking to local education and conservation partners — and keep the receipts public.

  4. 2023

    Beyond East Africa

    Dubai, Greece, Bali and Japan join the collection after years of guest requests and careful vetting.

  5. Today

    18 people, 12 destinations

    Still in Kampala, still answering our own phones, still planning every trip by hand.
