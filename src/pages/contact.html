Questions

Answers before you ask.

The things travellers ask us most. If yours isn't here, send a message — we'd rather answer it properly.

Ask on WhatsApp
How quickly will I hear back?

Within two working hours, Monday to Saturday, 8:00–19:00 EAT. Messages sent outside those hours get a reply first thing the next morning. If it's urgent, WhatsApp is fastest.

Do I pay a deposit to start planning?

No. We'll design a route and quote before you commit. A 30% deposit confirms your booking, with the balance due 30 days before departure. Flights and gorilla permits sometimes need earlier payment — we'll always flag that upfront.

Can you customise a listed tour?

Absolutely — that's the point. Every listed itinerary is a starting point. Add a beach extension, upgrade lodges, slow the pace, bring the grandparents. We'll re-quote and show you the difference.

What's included in the prices?

Prices are per person sharing and typically include accommodation, listed meals, private transport, guides, park fees and activities. International flights, visas, travel insurance and personal spending are excluded unless stated. Every quote itemises what's in and what's out.

Is Uganda safe for travellers?

The tourist areas we operate in are well travelled and welcoming. We monitor local advice, use vetted drivers and guides, and brief you before you go. As with any trip, sensible precautions and travel insurance matter — we'll help you prepare.

What if my plans change?

Date changes are free up to 30 days before departure, subject to supplier availability. Inside 30 days, supplier fees may apply. We'll always talk you through the options before anything is charged.

Privacy note

We collect only what we need to answer your enquiry: your name, contact details and trip notes. We never sell or rent your data. You can ask us to delete your details at any time by emailing bnasa77@gmail.com. Supplier bookings share only the details required to confirm your trip.
