Local insight, thoughtful travel

Local insight for more thoughtful travel.

Our Kampala base is a practical starting point for Uganda and East Africa travel. We discuss routes, suppliers and timing with local conditions in mind, and look for ways to make each trip more considerate.

  • Plan with local context

    We start with your route and timing, then coordinate with service providers who know the destination.

  • Travel with care

    We discuss ways to limit waste, respect wildlife and follow local guidance at the places you visit.

  • Keep benefits local

    We look for opportunities to work with local guides, drivers, stays and experience hosts.
